# Code of conduct

Samsarix CLI is maintained as a professional, welcoming open-source project. Participation in the
repository, issue tracker, reviews, and other project spaces requires respectful and constructive
conduct.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give and receive specific, actionable feedback in good faith.
- Credit other people's work and respect license, privacy, and confidentiality boundaries.
- Make space for different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Stop an interaction when another participant sets a reasonable boundary.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
impersonation, sustained disruption, or publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents Samsarix CLI or Samsarix LLC in a
public project-related setting. Report a possible violation privately to support@samsarix.com. Do
not use a public issue when doing so would expose personal or sensitive information.

Samsarix LLC maintainers will review reports in confidence to the extent practical. Depending on
severity and context, maintainers may provide guidance, remove content, limit participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant. Good-faith reports will not be
retaliated against. Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may themselves violate this policy.
